Hi, I am Isha Kaushik. I research the incentives, evidence and capital that builds long term health topics. Currently, building and testing hypergrowth models for the wellness industry.

Isha Kaushik

I’m half North and half South Indian, so I grew up with a fun mix of everything. I suppose that also meant learning pretty early to find my own way of doing useful things. Grew up meaning to be a clinician, then an inventor of therapies, until I repeatedly saw how painful the road still is from a breakthrough in the lab to the people who need it. Realized I’d rather spend a lifetime improving that journey - and now happily lose entire weekends to it. I favor simple things, and often put myself in non-obvious situations just to see how quickly I can find my way back to minimalism.

I recently earned my Master’s in Neuroscience from Columbia University (’26), supported by Founders and Michael Eisner Awards. My thesis evaluated deep-learning architectures for BCI thought-to-text, backed by a field audit and an early governance framework. Prior to that, I completed my Bachelor’s in Neuroscience at Amity University, Noida (’24), with minors in Medical Physiology and Psychology, as a merit scholar and silver medalist.

I’m probably an unreasonable fan of Jurassic Park. Besides developing an enduring soft spot for Ian Malcolm, the film left me rather interested in the ethics of scientific progress: just because we can do something, should we? That curiosity led me to explore law and ethics with Prof. Adriel Brown (Stanford Medicine), alongside Columbia Law School’s Law and Philosophy Programming (2025-26), followed by philosophy and education at Teachers College, where my supervised research became a paper on aesthetics, epistemic justice, and inventive pursuits. Outside academia, I’m also a trained Hindustani classical musician (Sangeet Prabhakar, 2021).

A few projects I’ve documented publicly span sleep research, gastrophysics, BCI, and nonprofit rebranding. My first two solo hackathon builds, JudgementOS (clinical reasoning) and ArcusOS (AI-native school), also earned honourable mentions at the NYEdtech Hackathon.

I’ve been lucky to learn from some brilliant people along the way. Sharing what I’ve picked up has become a big part of the experience, too. I’ve guest lectured on sleep neurobiology and the attention economy, mentored 100+ students from the Global South through Project EduAccess on bioscience research and ethics, and moderated global faculty panels debating gender equity in science at WiN.

Currently attempting to learn Mandarin through conversations on the streets of Chinatown. I speak Hindi (Khadi by birth, Braj by heart), English, Malayalam, and French.

Off the clock involves questionable experiments for pure sport, unnecessarily long debates on cultural philosophies in various circles of NYC, and a Substack draft that may or may not get finished.

Bonus points if your opening line involves Jurassic Park, Sherlock, or an obscure pop-culture reference. I’m always happy to entertain a good conversation. Email gets you to me fastest. Cheers!
