Project Title

A short 2-3 line brief describing the project

Introduction

Product: BehaveNeu NeuroBand – a luxury-grade, EEG-based Brain-Computer Interface (BCI) designed to enhance cognitive performance through real-time neurofeedback.

Purpose: Paired with a mobile platform, it offers personalized brain training by tracking user attention, emotion, and memory.

The Challenge: To evolve from premium neuro-gadget to validated cognitive tool, the NeuroBand needed rigorous neuroscience-backed testing, especially to assess its accuracy in capturing user responses to real-world creative stimuli like advertising.

Approach / Journey

The Role:

  • Synthesized insights from 50+ scientific papers to frame 3 testable hypotheses around attention, emotional resonance, and memory using the NeuroBand.
  • Designed psychophysics-based experiments (via PsychoPy) and curated 40+ ad stimuli to test cognitive response validity.
  • Collaborated with neurotech engineers to refine signal sensitivity, improving EEG accuracy by 30% for commercial use cases.

Solutions & Key Takeaways

Results:

  • Elevated NeuroBand’s credibility as a cognitive measurement tool for marketing and design applications.
  • Helped position BehaveNeu as a data-driven neurotech innovator ready for scalable industry deployment.
