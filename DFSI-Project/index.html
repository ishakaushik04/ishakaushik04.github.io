Project Title

A short 2-3 line brief describing the project

Introduction

Program: Columbia Entrepreneurship Design Studio & SIPA 2025 Cohort

Selected as one of 30 students from across Columbia University to collaborate with social enterprises, nonprofits, and public sector organizations on solving complex global challenges using human-centered design.

The Team: Five Columbia students from CEAS, Teachers College (me!), Social Work, and Columbia University Irving Medical Center.

The Client: Amber Waves, a women-led organization at the intersection of agriculture and education, running immersive programs to train future leaders in sustainable farming and food literacy.

The Problem: With the average U.S. farmer aged 58 and only 6% under 35, Amber Waves faced the challenge of enhancing its 8-month, 2,000-hour apprenticeship program to better train the next generation of sustainable farmers.

Approach / Journey

Phase 1: Discovery and Initial Hypothesis

We interviewed 30+ stakeholders within a week, from founders and apprentices to farm crew and local partners. I led qualitative data synthesis, turning interviews into 10+ detailed decks to align the team and sharpen problem framing. We scoped and co-developed a prototype for a unified data pipeline to streamline alumni tracking, outreach, and fundraising coordination. Initial testing didn’t move the needle on scalability.

Skills Applied: Stakeholder interviews, qualitative analysis, data synthesis, presentation design, cross-functional collaboration

Phase 2: Setback and Team Resilience

After three weeks stuck in doubts and debates, a fundraising event reignited our momentum. A hands-on team bonding workshop rebuilt trust, clarified roles, and strengthened collaboration.

Skills Applied: Conflict resolution, team communication

Phase 3: Reframing and Strategic Pivot

We discovered that public misperception of Amber Waves was the real issue. I led audience perception mapping, managed Excel workflows for outreach data, and built persona pipelines alongside empathy maps for three key segments. Engineers prototyped brand strategy tests, which drove a 40% increase in clicks from the target audience.

Skills Applied: Data management, persona development, empathy mapping, cross-functional prototype development

Phase 4: Scaling and Final Delivery

Scaling the rebrand required balancing community expansion with fundraising goals. I designed and presented a dual strategy targeting apprentices (20–35) and donors (100+ contributors/year), creating 5+ strategy decks to guide implementation. The founders responded enthusiastically, ready to adopt our recommendations immediately.

Skills Applied: Strategic planning, cross-functional collaboration, client communication, deadline management
